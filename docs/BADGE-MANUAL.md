# Hacker Badge Manual

Your badge is your ID, your game console, and your networking tool for the weekend. Short version of everything from opening ceremonies:

## Basics

- **Power:** switch at the top. If nothing happens, check the AA batteries in the back are in the right way. Turn it off when not using it — battery life is limited and low batteries cause glitches.
- **Buttons:** directionals on the left (navigate / play), **A** (action) and **B** (back) on the right, **Home** (all apps) and **Start** (launch / menus) at the bottom.
- **Your QR code** shows on boot. That's your ID for check-ins and food — don't leave your badge at your table. Wear your badge + wristband at all times. Phone ticket works for meals if your badge dies.

## Scan stickers

Find flower-pattern stickers around the venue → open the **Scanner** app → line up the scanner pattern at the bottom of your badge with the center of the sticker.

Scans unlock hidden apps, track sponsor visits, and collect workshop stamps. You start with a few apps — there are 4 pages to discover.

## Connect with people

1. Both of you finish onboarding, then open the **Connect** app from home.
2. Bump the sides of your badges together, held like a plus sign. No luck? Try again, or hold them side by side and hold **A** for 2 seconds.
3. View each other's profiles and QR contact info in the app.

Challenge: connect with at least 3 new people. Leave the **Mood** app on while mingling — green/blue LEDs mean "come say hi."

## Sync & back up

Sync backs up contacts and earns Goose Games quest points. Two ways:

- Plug into your computer → **Sync** tab on the Hacker Dashboard, or
- Open the **Sync** app at a sync station at the Badge Help Desk.

Turn the battery switch **OFF** before plugging into anything (computer, phone, battery bank).

## Solana scavenger hunt

Open the **Solana** app, visit their booth, and tap beacons around the event to unlock customizations for your badge ID page.

## Build your own app

Yes, you can write badge apps. Instructions: **badge.hackthenorth.com** (also printed on the back). Badge Workshop: Saturday 11:30 AM.

## Help

- **Badge Help Desk** in the QNX Makerspace (separate from the regular Help Desk), or **#hacker-badges** on Slack.
- Glitching? Probably dead batteries — come get fresh ones. You can also power the badge from a phone or battery bank (switch OFF first).

## Take care of it

No replacements if damaged. Keep it dry, don't crack the screen (removing the screen protector is fine), be gentle with the top switch, and take it off before sleep.

## Safety — read this

- If the top, back, or left side near the USB port feels **warm**: turn it off immediately, bring it to the Badge Help Desk.
- If it's **very hot or looks damaged**: turn it off, remove the batteries, bring it to us.
- **Before flying home:** remove the batteries and carry them separately. Badges are fine on planes, but fly battery-free.

Full details: **badge.hackthenorth.com**

Scan things. Find the hidden apps. Meet people. Build something. Just don't put it in the shower.
